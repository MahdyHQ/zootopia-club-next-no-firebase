## Master Prompt — Balanced Premium Version

```text
Transform the provided file into a handwritten-style educational infographic written entirely in English by following these guidelines:

1. Page Layout and Visual Appearance
- Background: Present the material on A4 notebook paper with horizontal lines, similar to a student's handwritten study notebook.
- Handwritten Style: Use a handwriting-like font that feels natural and slightly imperfect so it resembles genuine handwriting rather than a perfectly clean digital font.
- Organization: Arrange the information in clear, brief bullet points with sufficient spacing to make the notes easy to read and review quickly.

2. Color-Based Organization
- Blue Pen: Use this color for explanations, sequential steps, and general descriptive information.
- Red Marker (Bold): Use for main titles, section headings, and official definitions.
- Yellow Highlighter: Highlight essential terms, significant dates, and final results or answers.

3. Visual Elements
- Hand-Sketched Illustrations: Add simple diagrams, charts, maps, or flow diagrams placed in the middle or toward the right side of the page. Label them clearly and connect ideas with arrows where needed.
- Manual Emphasis: Draw rough circles, underlines, or marks by hand around key concepts to emphasize them.
- Margins: Decorate the margins with small hand-drawn doodles or sketches to give the notes a realistic and personalized notebook appearance.

4. Exam-Oriented Notes
- Important Indicators: Clearly mark content that is especially important for exams.
- Helpful Side Notes: Include small note boxes or bubbles labeled with phrases such as “Exam Tip,” “Common Error,” or “Memory Hint.”

5. Accuracy and Content Reliability
- Source Fidelity: Ensure that all information remains completely accurate and strictly derived from the uploaded file.
- No Changes to Content: Do not omit essential topics and do not add any information that does not appear in the original source.

Final Result
- The finished product should resemble high-quality scanned pages of handwritten study notes, designed specifically to help with reviewing and exam preparation.
- Write the final content entirely in English.
```
